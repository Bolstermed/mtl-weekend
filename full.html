Monday · September 7

Head Home

Pack up, check out of the villa by 11:00 AM, and the private van runs back to Montréal–Trudeau (YUL). The drive is about two hours and varies with traffic.

Recommended Departure

Plan a late-morning, midday or afternoon Monday departure unless the van plan is specifically arranged around an earlier one. Leave time for the drive, traffic, bags and border formalities.

Early-ish wake-upPackCheck out by 11 AM Private van to YUL~2 hr driveFly home
Why not an early flight?
Tremblant → YUL is roughly two hours before you reach the terminal, so a 9 AM flight would mean a 5 AM van. Book midday or later.
